🎯 What is IntraSocks?

IntraSocks is a powerful yet easy-to-use tool that acts as a middleman between your computer and the internet. It caches web pages as you browse, meaning once you visit a site, you can view it again even without an internet connection. Think of it as your personal web library that saves everything you see for later.

But that's not all! It also gives you a floating, draggable dashboard that shows you live network traffic — every file your computer requests and receives from websites. You can see images, scripts, styles, and more, all in real-time. This makes IntraSocks perfect for anyone who wants to understand how websites work, developers who need to debug, or travelers who need offline access to important documents.

💡 How It Works

IntraSocks uses a man-in-the-middle (MitM) proxy approach. When you connect to a website, IntraSocks intercepts the connection, captures the data, and stores a copy in its local database. At the same time, it lets the original data pass through to your browser, so you never notice any difference — except for the fact that you can now view everything offline!
